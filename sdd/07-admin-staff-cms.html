Admin / staff CMS

Document: SDD-07
Status: Working draft
Date: 18 September 2026

1. Purpose

Staff tools for running the campus: Registry, Faculty, Bursary/Finance, Quality Assurance, Marketing, and remaining operational desks. Build new screens only for verified gaps. If the old CMS already runs the workflow, the portal may deep-link or omit the screen and still mark the capability Live once verified.

2. Design rule (CAP-44)

Confirm existing-CMS staff access for admissions first. A new administrator chrome is not a prerequisite for M2 if Registry can already issue offers in the old CMS.

3. Department map

flowchart LR
  MKT[Marketing<br/>communications] -->|handoff| REG[Registry<br/>decisions and records]
  REG --> FAC[Faculty<br/>schedule and curriculum]
  REG --> BUR[Bursary<br/>student ledger]
  BUR --> FIN[Finance<br/>reconciliation]
  FAC --> QA[QA<br/>approvals and evidence]
  REG --> OPS[Support desks<br/>visa housing requests]

4. Registry

IDs Milestone Staff job
CAP-03, 05, 06, 07 M2 Review evidence, qualification/credits, foreign equivalency, offer, first enrolment
CAP-08, 09 M3 Student master, emergency contacts, document permissions
CAP-10 M3 Configure and approve semester registration
CAP-13 M3 Approve and release results
CAP-16, 55 M3 Announce and publish policies (campus requested)
CAP-15 M5 Graduation and official transcripts

Rules:

  • Marketing publishes recruitment copy; Registry owns formal application decisions.
  • Foreign awards are never auto-equivalent (CAP-05, 06).
  • Result release is a Registry/exams action; student GPA views must not show unreleased marks.

5. Faculty

IDs Milestone Staff job
CAP-11 M3 Schedule classes, lecturers, rooms (campus requested)
CAP-14, 23 M3 Study plans, prerequisites, outcomes
CAP-22 M3 Past-paper publication rights (campus requested)
CAP-24 M5 Exam administration and approved arrangements

The lecturer portal is the day-to-day teaching surface; Faculty staff own the timetable grid and programme rules.

6. Bursary and Finance

IDs Milestone Staff job
CAP-45 M3 Invoices, balances, payment records
CAP-46 M3 Verify bank-transfer proofs; allocate; Finance reconciles
CAP-47 M5 Online payment reconciliation and financial documents
CAP-48 M5 Scholarships and incentives

Rule: a student proof (CAP-46) does not change the ledger until Bursary verifies it. Finance owns reconciliation and official documents; Bursary owns the student account handoff.

7. Quality Assurance

IDs Milestone Staff job
CAP-04 M2 Institution, programme, delivery-site approvals for intended campuses
CAP-42, 43 M3 Copyright/provider permissions; material approval; LMS-readiness evidence
CAP-30, 40 M5 Evaluations, confidentiality, institutional analytics

CAP-04 is an admissions-launch dependency: do not take applications for a programme/site that is not recorded as approved.

8. Marketing

CAP-16 (M2): publish recruitment and applicant communications. No status change on applications.

10. Current baseline

No staff application. Suggested next-action roles in BASE-* rows are hints, not named owners. Every staff row is Not started except CAP-36 Not applicable.

11. Acceptance

  • M2: Registry can complete application-to-enrolment on verified old CMS or new screens; CAP-04 evidence collected for Cyberjaya programmes.
  • M3: Registration, result release, billing verification, timetable administration, and content approval each have a named Live path.
  • Do not open a parallel staff UI that double-enters data the old CMS already holds.
