# Academic Graduation — CAP-15

CAP-15 is a session-only frontend demonstration of graduation clearance, Finance settlement, optional convocation and collection. `/academic/graduation` is the progress hub inside the existing Academic heading, student summary and tabs. It reuses Online Forms for submissions/messages, Finance for invoices and payment records, and the linked CAP-50 cancellation case for an international graduate's passport progress. Refresh resets the mock session. No live Registry, Finance, EMGS or Malaysian Immigration processing is implied.

## Layout and navigation

- Reuse the Study Plan and Finance composition: `SummaryCard`, panel `ContentSection`, `InnerSection`, `DataTable`, status badges and standard buttons, with 32px major-section spacing. A full-width `SegmentedTabs` navigator divides the eligible journey into four numbered steps plus a separate final **Complete** page: Step 1 Clearance, Step 2 Graduation fee, Step 3 Convocation, Step 4 Documents/student pass and Complete.
- Each step owns its contextual status summary and related named sections. Completed steps show a check icon in the navigator. A route without an explicit step opens the next incomplete step and opens Complete only after Step 4 finishes; `?step=clearance|fee|convocation|collection|complete` supports direct selection and browser Back/Forward, while invalid values fall back to the recommended page. A submitted or under-review clearance uses the warning SummaryCard treatment, including after all department checks clear, until Registry gives its final approval. Only approved clearance uses success styling and provides **Proceed to Step 2**.
- The Clearance summary keeps academic completion, Registry clearance and the submitted form ID in its columns. Its right-side action slot presents a large solid status Badge. After all department checks pass but before Registry approval, the warning card reads **Pending final approval**. When a process exists, its third flush-divided SummaryCard section contains the department-clearance table, whose status badges use the solid semantic variants. An empty department note follows the actual status: pending reads **Awaiting department review**, cleared reads **Department clearance confirmed**, and action-required reads **Student action required**. The edge-to-edge action footer follows Eligibility and clearance. Once approved, it keeps secondary **View Graduation Clearance Form** beside primary **Proceed to Step 2**; the separate Clearance application card is not rendered.
- Graduation fee uses one SummaryCard: its large solid payment-status Badge occupies the action slot and Invoice total is a divided summary column. While a transfer proof is pending, the single **Pending validation** Badge remains in the summary action slot and a third flush-divided section explains that Finance validation has not settled the invoice. The action footer keeps **View Graduation Invoice** primary while payment is outstanding; after settlement that link becomes secondary beside the primary **Proceed to Step 3** action.
- Convocation uses the same summary anatomy: the large Badge reads **Not started** until a form exists and, after an attending response is submitted, reads **Under review** while the summary adds Attendance, Gown size and Convocation form ID columns. Ceremony guidance is a separate borderless raised `InnerSection` below the summary. The edge-to-edge footer owns **Open/View Convocation Form** and adds **Proceed to Step 4** after Registry confirms an attending response. A submitted **Not attending** response completes this optional step immediately, uses the success treatment and opens Step 4 without an unnecessary Registry confirmation; withdrawal still returns the journey to Step 3.
- Step 4 separates two independently color-coded main SummaryCards. The Documents card owns its large progress Badge, academic/Registry/location columns and the Documents table in its third flush-divided section. Its untouched state uses the neutral default surface, active preparation/collection uses warning and full collection uses success regardless of passport progress. International students receive a second Student pass SummaryCard projected from the linked CAP-50 graduation-triggered cancellation. It remains warning-colored through passport handover and cancellation, then turns green only after passport return. Its heading and one-line description explain the student's next action or what the university will do next for the current passport state.
- Once every applicable Step 4 condition passes, the route defaults to the separate **Complete** tab. Its default neutral SummaryCard places a green checked icon above **Congratulations!**, followed by recorded milestone dates and a **Download Transcript** action that fills the footer width on phones; the completed Step 4 cards remain available on their own tab.
- Students who are not eligible see the compact Academic `EmptyState` used for completed Module Registration, with the unmet-requirements explanation and a **View Study Plan** link. Eligible students see the progress hub sections. Completed students without a current semester retain the Academic/Profile shell with **No current semester**; historical selectors default to the latest owned semester. Academic tabs scroll to reveal the active tab when Community reduces the available width.
- An eligible completed student starts with one active Graduation Clearance account issue. A student with one completed Programme Enrolment does not receive the `ProgrammeContextBar` on Dashboard, Graduation, Finance, Profile, or any other route; students with multiple Programme Enrolments retain the selector so they can change the owned route context. The shared header **Action required** control opens Profile, where the issue appears as an orange warning card and opens the exact owned Graduation Clearance form. Submitting that form resolves the account issue and removes it from the header and Profile active-issue grid without restoring the completed-enrolment strip.
- Study Plan provides **Open Graduation** only after its four programme requirements pass and links to `/academic/graduation`, allowing the hub to select the recommended current step. Direct `/academic/graduation#documents` navigation remains supported for an already available Step 4. Document rows show status and recorded collection dates. The completed Graduation view restores **Download Transcript** as an enabled visual prototype action matching the approved reference, but it remains an intentional no-op: no file, resource URL, browser download or document-delivery backend is implemented. The discounted-course offer remains omitted.
- Loading preserves the step-navigation and summary geometry. Read-error/retry and unconfigured-campus states retain the Academic shell and panel. Step changes, the document anchor and browser Back/Forward remain within the same session.

## Eligibility and clearance

Starting and submitting Graduation Clearance both require a fully completed Study Plan: every required semester completed with published term results, every curriculum module passed, sufficient earned credits, minimum published CGPA, zero outstanding balance, and the existing applicable passport/visa coverage requirement. A draft does not bypass a later failed eligibility check. Student Pass applicability is derived from citizenship against the exact enrolment campus, and ownership is resolved through that enrolment.

One student/enrolment/template has at most one draft and one submitted, under-review or completed application. Repeated Start resumes the draft. Rejected or withdrawn cases may be replaced when entry requirements still pass; their snapshots, messages and process records remain historical. Submitted answers and embedded documents never reopen for editing.

Submission creates one `GraduationClearance` record linked to the exact student, enrolment, campus and clearance submission and resolves the matching active Graduation Clearance account issue. It does not approve graduation or issue an invoice. Faculty, Finance and Library checks are reviewed separately. Finance must record its own clearance decision even when the eligibility projection reports no outstanding balance. The account balance remains a separate eligibility fact and is rechecked at final Registry approval. Information requests become reviewer messages on the existing case.

Registry approval requires all three department checks and every eligibility check. The mock transaction stages and validates the complete change before publishing it: one completed clearance case, one approval timestamp, one globally numbered Finance invoice, its charge line and its immutable Service Tax snapshot. Invalid, duplicate or stale approvals do not partially write an invoice or decision.

Approved clearance is retained when its new invoice creates a balance or the subsequent passport process advances. These are later tasks; the hub does not return the student to initial eligibility. Academic completion is derived from published study history and stays separate from administrative approval and collection.

The normal final-semester boundary is modeled as one session-only canonical transition. It publishes the exact final Assessment, Module and Term Results, completes every final-period Module Registration, changes the retained final Study Period from `current` to `completed`, closes the Academic Term, and then changes the owning Programme Enrolment from `active` to `completed`. The source graph is unchanged when validation fails. A completed student therefore has no current Study Period, while all six historical Study Period records and published results remain available to Academic reads. The transition requires all earlier periods to be complete, one passing result for every curriculum module, the required earned credits, and the minimum CGPA before Graduation eligibility can become available.

## Graduation fee and Finance

- The approved sample **base fee is RM500**, for both attending and non-attending graduates. The sample FeeItem is classified `taxable-education`; this is a prototype assumption requiring campus Finance/tax confirmation.
- Existing issuance-time SST rules apply. Nadia's Malaysian-campus, non-Malaysian-citizen example has RM30 SST and RM530 due. A Malaysian citizen is exempt under the existing fixture rules. Later payments and proof reviews never recalculate the tax snapshot.
- Students use existing Finance bank-transfer instructions/proof submission or counter instructions. Pending proof does not reduce debt. A development-only verification creates one confirmed Payment and exact invoice Allocation; partial payments are supported. A confirmed payment without an invoice allocation remains visible as unapplied credit and does not satisfy Finance clearance or unlock later Graduation steps.
- Verified and rejected proofs retain review timestamps, reasons and, for verification, the Payment ID. Replacing/withdrawing a pending proof preserves reviewed history. Overpayments, foreign invoices, duplicate payment references, consumed/obsolete proofs and future payment dates are rejected. A counter payment invalidates a pending proof that exceeds the new remaining balance, retaining its reason.
- Finance developer actions work across every scenario student's open invoices. Each proof or invoice choice identifies its student owner and updates that exact ledger; it does not add a payment gateway.

## Convocation, documents and student pass

After approval, full graduation-invoice payment and a settled account, **Convocation Registration** becomes available as the sixth Online Forms template. Attendance is `attending` or `not-attending`. Attending requires a gown size (XS–XXL); an optional note is available. Shared select fields and conditional requirements are validated in both the form and API. An attending registration is submitted immutably and can be confirmed by the developer Registry simulation. A submitted non-attending response needs no staff confirmation and immediately satisfies the optional Convocation step; because its case remains open, a permitted withdrawal removes that decision and returns the journey to Step 3. Corrections use Messages. Completed graduation and attending Convocation cases continue accepting messages; other closed forms remain read-only.

Document preparation is independent of ceremony attendance. Registry advances each of Completion letter, Transcript and Scroll through not started → preparing → ready for collection → collected. Each transition requires approved clearance and a settled account; release is an explicit Registry step, and collection records an ISO timestamp. No document body, file delivery or collection appointment is fabricated.

For international students only, Registry approval atomically creates or reuses the CAP-50 graduation-triggered cancellation for the exact Student Visa / Pass. The case is fee-waived, has no duplicate Online Form or Immigration invoice, and begins at accepted documents. Graduation projects it as awaiting handover → passport received → cancellation processing → cancellation completed → ready for collection → passport returned. Cancellation completion marks the matching Student Visa / Pass `cancelled`, records its cancellation time, preserves its original expiry, and drives the destructive Profile and Immigration presentation while the International Office prepares it for collection; the separate ready-for-collection state tells the student to follow its collection instructions. Because the enrolment is completed and the trigger is Graduation, the portal does not offer self-service reapplication. It progresses independently of document readiness. No actual government cancellation is asserted. Campus-local students have no passport record or workflow. The final success summary requires approved clearance, settled account, all documents collected and, where applicable, returned passport. Academic completion, clearance and collection dates remain visible.

## Data and developer boundary

`PortalApi.getAcademicGraduation()` returns a Zod-validated `AcademicGraduationResponse` under the scenario/session- and Programme-Enrolment-scoped Graduation query. It resolves the exact owned `programmeEnrolmentId` selected by the route; without one, the shared deterministic default chooses active, then the newest deferred, completed, or withdrawn enrolment. Unknown students, foreign or missing enrolments, and multiple simultaneously active enrolments fail explicitly. Campus/programme configuration never falls back across campuses, and inactive contexts never receive active-only mutations.

The canonical graph adds campus/programme-version `CampusGraduationConfiguration` records and enrolment-owned `GraduationClearance` records with department checks, invoice/submission references, document progress and a nullable relationship to the CAP-50 Immigration case. Data Model Explorer entries describe the logical frontend model, including the derived Graduation response, Immigration relationship and reviewed Finance proofs; this is not a prescribed production database schema.

The new **Graduation ready / Nadia Pratama** scenario has a separate synthetic 2023 curriculum enrolment, six completed semesters, 120 earned credits, published 3.00 CGPA, an empty settled Finance account and valid initial passport/pass coverage. It has no current semester and starts without a clearance application, with one active Graduation Clearance account issue linking Profile to the exact form. Alya and Rizal retain their existing academic, fee and registration data. Read projections accept completed enrolments; registration and other restricted student mutations retain active/current ownership gates.

`/dev/admin-actions` is a narrow all-student session simulation outside the production-facing `PortalApi` interface. Its implementation is dynamically imported only in DEV and excluded from production assets. A student selector lists every scenario student and defaults to a student with a graduation process. Pending transfer proofs and counter-payment invoice choices show their owners. The currently viewed student does not filter the review workspace; **Open Student Graduation** changes the student view before opening that student's hub.

In development, the student portal sidebar footer exposes **Admin Actions** as an in-app route. Use that control after submitting Graduation Clearance or a Finance transfer proof so navigation retains the current mock session. The Admin Actions body owns its vertical scrollbar within the viewport-locked developer shell, so Convocation, document and passport controls remain reachable. Typing a `/dev/...` URL into the address bar or refreshing the page reloads the frontend and resets session-only submissions, proofs and review outcomes.

The workspace supports graduation department checks, information requests/rejection/approval, attending-convocation confirmation, document transitions, linked CAP-50 passport transitions, proof review and counter payments. The Convocation confirmation control remains unavailable for non-attending responses because they need no staff decision. It exposes no generic production approval controls for other Online Forms. Every command carries the target student ID and checks exact student, enrolment, campus, invoice/proof, Graduation process and Immigration case ownership, plus revisions and session generation. Scenario switching clears student query caches while retaining shared canonical Graduation, Immigration and Finance records in the same mock session. Switching or resetting still rejects late responses before UI success callbacks; refresh creates a new session.

## Research and assumptions

The approved design separates clearance, payment, optional convocation and collection. [UTP clearance](https://ucs.utp.edu.my/knowledgebase/article/KA-01252/en-us) describes an online request with departmental checks; [UTP convocation](https://utp.microsoftcrmportals.com/knowledgebase/article/KA-01292/en-us) provides an attending/non-attending reference. These inform the prototype and are not verified Limkokwing policy. A current public Limkokwing Malaysia clearance procedure was not verified during planning.

The three department gates, RM500 base fee, taxable-education classification, gown choices, Registry counter location and document sequence are synthetic campus assumptions. Student Pass cancellation ordering is documented with [CAP-50 Immigration](immigration.md) and requires campus confirmation. Ceremony dates, venue, guest allocation, gown collection/deposit/return rules, approved legal/immigration instructions, policy documents and production fees require campus confirmation. Production staff authorization/audit, payment reconciliation, live EMGS/Immigration work, passport custody, persistent records, document preparation/delivery and physical-device certification remain deferred. No live checklist or dated planning baseline is changed by this implementation.

## Verification

Focused service and browser coverage exercises completed/blocked eligibility, missing ownership, campus isolation, local/international branches, draft/resume, immutable submissions/messages, rejection/withdrawal/reapplication, approval and invoice atomicity, SST, pending/rejected/verified proofs, partial/counter payments, duplicate/stale actions, attendance/gown validation, independent collection/passport progress and final completion. Existing desktop checks cover 1280px and 1440px with Community open, closed and resized; the responsive matrix covers the Graduation shell, scrollable step navigation, compact records, stacked actions, keyboard activation, route history, and overflow at supported narrower widths. Production scanning checks that developer command implementation is absent. Actual gate results and warnings are recorded in [handoff](handoff.md).

## Presentation statuses vs API statuses

Graduation URL steps (`clearance` | `fee` | `convocation` | `collection` | `complete`) and UI badge labels such as **Not started**, **Pending final approval**, **Pending validation**, and **Approved** (for Online Form `completed`) are frontend presentation only. They are not PortalApi enums. Wire statuses remain the Zod contracts on the Graduation response, Online Forms, Finance invoices/proofs, and the projected Student Pass statuses below.

## Immigration → Graduation Student Pass projection

Graduation projects a graduation-triggered Immigration cancellation onto `process.studentPass.status` using the shared map in `src/contracts/immigration-graduation-projection.ts`. Immigration case `completed` maps to Graduation `passport-returned`. Admin Actions that advance Graduation passport controls write the inverse Immigration case status.
