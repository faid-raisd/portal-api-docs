# Campus Services

## Current scope

Services is a read-only campus-information feature. Cyberjaya supplies six populated categories adapted from Limkokwing's Experience > Services website: Transportation, Dining, Wellness, Connectivity, Shopping and Recreation. The portal also includes the campus-owned **Accessibility Support** placeholder after Wellness, bringing the current catalogue to seven entries. Accommodation is not part of the release surface: the retired `/accommodation` route replaces to Dashboard, while `/services/accommodation`, `/services/finance` and other removed or unknown service paths replace to Transportation. `/finance` remains the student's account, invoices, payments and awards. Community service chats remain separate and do not include Accommodation.

There are no bookings, purchases, applications, payment actions, Wi-Fi configuration actions, contact-message sending, forms, mutations, uploads, live website calls, browser persistence or backend integrations.

`TabbedPagePanelBanner` displays each page title and introduction over its lead image, directly beneath the tabs. It is a fixed 288px high with no outer inset or individual radius. The image covers the banner using centred cropping; text is vertically centred with fixed 32px insets, a fixed 208px desktop column, and a fixed 16px title/description gap. These media dimensions do not grow when a user enlarges text; overflowing copy instead scrolls inside a keyboard-focusable region. Titles use 24px `type-section-lg`; descriptions use 14px `type-body`. Cyberjaya's six source-backed banner descriptions use three authored lines so their wrapping is intentional rather than dependent on panel width. Dark text is used for Transportation, Dining, Connectivity and Shopping; Wellness and Recreation use light text. Gradients default to off. Transportation and Shopping explicitly enable light gradients; the other source-backed pages remain gradient-free. Absent or failed media keeps the same height and copy on a portal surface using normal text tokens.

`TabbedPagePanel` now composes the shared `PagePanelLayout` used by Announcement details. Services selects its no-inset content mode, allowing `ServiceContent` to own the existing 60px wide-layout body padding directly without the former negative-margin cancellation. The body inherits the shared panel surface instead of drawing a second rounded gradient container below the banner. `ServiceContent` renders the source body through feature-owned `ServicesSectionContent`, without repeating the unchanged banner. Composed sections support headings inside nested stack/two-column groups, paragraphs, lists, notes, photo groups, dish cards, illustrated benefit cards and semantic tables. Layout configuration belongs to canonical campus content, never route-slug conditionals. `ContentSection` remains the fallback for standard sections; `CardGrid` and `DataTable` are reused with feature-scoped styles. The department contact block and its photo/icons are not rendered. The optional contact API contract remains for compatibility; the Cyberjaya fixture image is null.

The content region follows source 60px wide insets, 32px column gaps and dividers. Below a 640px **container** width it uses the same 16px content inset as Announcement details and the source phone stacking. Photos reserve 360px on wide containers and 220px on narrow containers, using cover cropping; split photos stretch with their adjacent copy. Dining cards change from three columns to one. Missing images retain their dimensions and copy. These container rules operate inside the portal-wide responsive shell and also remain inspectable through the dev-only isolated canvas.

## Navigation and states

Two-column information tables, including hours and rates, wrap both columns within the phone content area. Tables with more columns retain their focused horizontal scroll region and 560px minimum width. A simple hours table must not hide its second column behind unnecessary horizontal scrolling.

- Services uses the Profile/Academic sidebar disclosure. Children, tabs and breadcrumbs derive from the campus catalogue. Child activation compares service URLs rather than the shared `services` page identity. Categories are fixture content, not a routing enum.
- Accessibility Support uses `/services/accessibility` and sits after Wellness. Its neutral no-image banner and shared empty-content treatment explain only that campus information, contacts and guidance will be published. CAP-51 adds **Open Accessibility Support Form**, a campus-scoped link to the Online Forms template without starting a draft. The form opens in the separate Online Forms shell; the Services body contains no form inputs or Community contact. Campus policies, guidance and live staff operations remain outstanding.
- `/services` and unknown or removed service paths replace the current history entry with the first catalogue page, currently Transportation. Ordering is `displayOrder`, then English title and stable ID. Valid direct routes retain normal Back/Forward history.
- Campuses without pages show `No services published` at `/services`; stale child paths replace to that root. Missing relevant enrolments produce a null campus and empty collections. Cyberjaya is never a fallback.
- Catalogue loading retains the heading and matching panel skeleton. Read errors offer Try again. Detail loading retains the selected tab with a matching 288px banner skeleton. Errors and unavailable details retain a title-only fallback banner above their body state. Missing details use an in-panel empty state.
- The tab row scrolls horizontally when necessary, with the existing `scrollbar-hidden` utility hiding only its scrollbar. Selection, keyboard focus and Community resizing bring the active/focused tab into view. Existing gesture deduplication, indicator motion, panel insets and focus treatment remain.
- A new tab creates one history entry. Current-tab/sidebar reselection and Back/Forward reset the main scroller. Local tab scrolling does not navigate.

## Canonical content and API

`CampusServicePageRecord` has a stable ID, campus ID, campus-unique slug, title, display order, introduction, nullable lead image, banner appearance and ordered sections. A published catalogue record may intentionally have a null image and an empty sections array while administrators prepare approved campus content; the renderer uses one reusable empty-content state for that case. The validated `banner` object supplies `textVariant` (`dark` or `light`) and `gradient` (boolean); omitted values normalize to dark and false, including records created before the banner addition. Appearance belongs to the campus page and is returned only by the existing detail API, without a route-name lookup or new endpoint. Blocks support paragraphs, headings, notes, lists, image groups, rectangular tables with stable row IDs, and recursive stack/two-column groups. Optional configuration controls composed section headings, dividers, responsive order, media presentation/crops/stretching and table column proportions. Nested identities validate across each page. React renders plain text. Images require a local path or HTTPS URL, alternative text and positive integer dimensions. Optional responsive variants each carry source, width and height; sources and candidate widths are unique and aspect ratios agree. Shared `ResponsiveMedia` measures rendered width with ResizeObserver for browser srcset selection, including Community changes. Banners are eager; body images are lazy.

`CampusServiceContactRecord` retains one optional contact per campus for API compatibility, without rendering it in Services. Address, phone and email may be null; hours may be empty; its optional nullable image carries validated source, alternative text and reserved dimensions. Presence in the canonical page collection represents published content; unpublished content is outside this frontend contract.

- `PortalApi.getServices (`POST /v1/portal/getServices`)` / `PortalApi.getServices()` returns validated `ServicesResponse`: nullable campus context and ordered summaries.
- `PortalApi.getServicePage (`POST /v1/portal/getServicePage`)` / `PortalApi.getServicePage({ slug })` returns validated `ServicePageResponse`: campus context, nullable page and nullable contact. Input slugs validate with Zod.
- Both projections start with the stable selected student ID and follow the exact URL-backed Programme Enrolment to its owning campus. When the route omits an enrolment ID, the shared selector uses its documented deterministic default. Students cannot select a foreign enrolment or arbitrary campus. Alya and Rizal both see Cyberjaya information.
- Queries use the existing runtime cache scope; detail keys include the slug. Scenario replacement clears both caches. Parsed responses cannot mutate the underlying graph.
- Graph validation rejects absent campus owners, repeated IDs, duplicate campus/slug pairs and multiple contacts per campus. Explorer tables, campus relationships, derived catalogue rows, endpoint metadata and per-student campus filtering are synchronized.

## Source inventory and adaptations

[The source manifest](services-source-manifest.json) preserves the six active Services website sources plus the separately retained, retired Finance source capture and their archived original checksums. The active delivery inventory contains 34 images and 68 WebP variants. Its `activeCategories` lists the six imported categories; `portalPlaceholderCategories` records Accessibility separately so the manifest does not imply source copy or imagery. Accommodation source records and media are removed. Originals and reviewed restoration masters live outside public under `assets/services/`. The retired Finance/contact PNGs are excluded from public delivery. `npm run optimize:services-assets` reproducibly uses Sharp to resize and encode reviewed masters: photos/banners at 1600/800 long edge, at most 250,000/100,000 bytes; dishes at 640/320, at most 60,000/25,000 bytes. It preserves original aspect ratios and records dimensions, quality, bytes, checksums and individual restoration/fallback notes. All 34 active images receive a built-in AI editing attempt and visual comparison; visible failures are retried, with optimized-original fallbacks recorded where fidelity cannot be retained. All assets remain local at runtime.

[The layout inventory](services-layout-reference.md) and [captured text/image inventory](services-layout-reference.json) record the remaining source hierarchy, full body text and media arrangements reviewed on 6 September 2026. Body text is restored with obvious spelling corrections only. Existing compact banners remain intentionally adapted. Table semantics and whitespace are normalized; the unexplained `GB Students` label remains. Both approximate and detailed source library counts are retained. Dining’s outlet image mapping now follows the retrieved CSS and visual reference.

This remains a website snapshot: rates, hours, journey durations and historical collection counts have not been independently confirmed with operators. The source intermittently returned 522 errors, but all six active source pages and the separately retained Finance capture were successfully inspected. Source-specific differences intentionally retained as adaptations are portal typography/dark tokens, existing banners, the container breakpoint, and the approved removal of Accommodation, Finance and department contacts.

## Verification

The Node-based `npm run check:services-assets` gate verifies WebP decoding, byte budgets, checksums, dimensions, original provenance and the exact public asset inventory; unit tests verify every imported image is used.

Browser tests cover all seven direct portal routes, source-backed images, history, removed-route redirects, scroll reset, keyboard access, Community dock/overlay behavior, loading/errors/retry, empty sections/campuses, long text and image failure across the supported portal widths. Isolated browser coverage still checks the six source-backed bodies at 375, 390, 430, 639, 640 and 768px, including image ordering, photo geometry, column counts, image failures and overflow. Source-copy tests compare every captured website body widget against rendered canonical content, including figures and captions. The Accessibility placeholder has no website-copy comparison. Run the full quality gate and change-surface review before release.
