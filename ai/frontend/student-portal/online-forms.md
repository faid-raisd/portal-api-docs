# Online Forms (CAP-51)

On phone layouts, preparation guidance links wrap within their card rather than clipping at the panel edge. Prefilled records use the shared stacked `DetailField` treatment, and category segments grow with their coarse-pointer targets while keeping long labels clear of their dividers. The selected category is restored into view after Safari page restoration or a late layout resize. These presentation rules preserve all existing submission and case ownership behavior.

Online Forms is the responsive student frontend for campus-owned forms, drafts, submission tracking and case conversations. It replaces the Requests placeholder. All changes are held in the mock Portal API session and reset on refresh. Changing the development student view retains each student's owned form records while clearing scoped UI queries. No staff portal or production submission service is included.

## Pages and references

`/online-forms` has Available Forms and My Forms tabs. Category and selected-tab state live in the URL. Available Forms uses a shared 288px banner with local 1600px/800px WebP variants, full-width segmented category tabs and a reusable linked-list composition. Every available row is one keyboard-accessible route link with a form icon, concise title/description and decorative chevron; unavailable forms remain readable, use an italic eligibility explanation and do not expose a false link. The category segments show dividers between inactive neighbours, but never beside the selected segment. My Forms lists drafts and submissions with references, statuses, update dates and a Messages column that calls out unseen reviewer updates; the row remains the single Resume/View target. The unread reviewer `IssueCard` appears only on the Available Forms and My Forms list pages and is hidden from template and submission details. `/online-forms/forms/:formId` reads a template without creating a draft. It labels its immediate breadcrumb as **Form Details**, presents the read-only **Prefilled Details** in a bordered `DetailSectionCard`, then places **Before you start**, its explanatory copy, and any required-document list in one raised, borderless `InnerSection`; eligibility guidance and the separate bordered Start/Resume action bar follow it. The action bar uses the shared edge-to-edge divider so it flushes to the containing panel while its button remains inset. `/online-forms/submissions/:submissionId` shows its editor or submitted details. `/requests` replaces history with `/online-forms`.

The **Medical Screening Result** form is not a ninth catalogue choice. Immigration Step 4 exposes it only for the exact active new-pass case after eVAL approval. It uses the same template, draft, save-and-close, resume, confirmation, immutable submission, withdrawal, status history, and review presentation as every other Online Form. Its single required upload accepts PDF, JPEG, or PNG, and the draft snapshots the exact `immigrationCaseId`.

All four student scenarios receive their owned Cyberjaya templates. Other campuses receive no Cyberjaya fallback. New Student Pass requires an international enrolment without a current pass, or a completed student-requested cancellation on the same active eligible enrolment. Renewal and cancellation require the exact current enrolment-owned valid or expiring pass. Expired passes, graduation or university-directed cancellations, inactive enrolments, and statutory revocation require International Office review. Only one active CAP-50 case is permitted. Academic Appeal requires an owned published result. Graduation Clearance requires all CAP-15 entry checks at both Start and Submit: completed published curriculum, earned credits, minimum CGPA, zero existing debt and applicable pass coverage. Rejected/withdrawn attempts can be replaced when current eligibility permits, but duplicate active or approved cases are blocked. Questions, routing departments and eligibility are synthetic fixture assumptions requiring campus approval.

The catalogue supplies its campus timezone independently of its template collection, so My Forms retains the correct local timestamps even when all templates are unpublished. Student Pass records explicitly reference their Programme Enrolment; the synthetic Rizal record belongs to his Cyberjaya enrolment. A transferred student cannot use a pass from a different enrolment to start renewal or reapplication. Renewal and cancellation prefills use the student-facing **Student Pass number** and **Student Pass expiry** labels. Every New Student Pass template places the outside-Malaysia requirement and official guidance link inside its existing **Before you start** card; the International Office verifies the condition during review, so no self-attested location field is added. This linkage and copy record prototype ownership and workflow assumptions rather than establishing government eligibility.

## Draft and submission rules

- Single-page grouped forms save drafts in the current session. Reopening resumes the matching draft, including unsent edits and selected-file metadata/previews. Save and close waits for the current draft save. Failed saves retain work and allow retry.
- Prefills derive from selected student → exact selected Programme Enrolment → campus/programme, with the latest Personal Profile overlay for current contact details. Published-result choices retain exact registration/attempt ownership. System-owned values are read-only.
- Catalogue, My Forms, detail, unread acknowledgement, and case-message reads retain that `programmeEnrolmentId`; cases from another owned programme never enter the selected list. Ordinary inactive-enrolment forms are read-only. A completed enrolment may still start the exact Graduation Clearance or Convocation form when CAP-15 eligibility permits; this narrow exception does not reopen generic, Immigration, or current-study forms.
- Form definitions describe typed fields rather than importing HTML. Upload fields occur only where a template asks for a document. There is no student Supporting documents section.
- Embedded files reuse the attachment limits: 25 MiB per file, five files and 75 MiB per field, with one file for each CAP-50 passport, photograph, or medical-result field. A form field may further restrict accepted MIME types; the medical-result field accepts PDF, JPEG, or PNG. Existing Finance and assignment limits remain separate. `FileUploadField` keeps uploaded filenames, sizes, raised file tiles, replace/remove controls, and multiple-file rows inside the dashed container; its underlined replacement control does not repeat university-source or selection labels, and guidance/errors follow the shared 8px field rhythm.
- CAP-50 context responses expose selected-student evidence references for upload fields. Draft creation takes an immutable evidence snapshot. A required document is satisfied by its owned snapshot or an uploaded replacement; missing records render the ordinary chooser. The replacement belongs only to that submission and never overwrites Profile records.
- Validate complete answers, required files, eligibility and ownership before confirmation. Cancellation or failure retains the draft. Submission atomically assigns a reference, locks a snapshot of the definition/prefills/answers/files and adds history. Duplicate submission cannot create another case.
- Submitted fields/files never unlock for the student. They remain in the original field controls with disabled presentation; additional information is sent through case messages. Student submission never changes official marks, visa validity or account balances. Clearance and Immigration submissions create their administrative session records; the separate development simulation controls review, fee issuance and later progress.
- Draft, Submitted, Under review, Completed, Rejected and Withdrawn are the supported statuses. Submitted form summaries use a faded warning, success, or destructive surface for Under review, Completed, or Rejected respectively, paired with the matching solid white-text status Badge. Seeded outcomes can describe approval/resolution. Open cases permit confirmed withdrawal; terminal cases are read-only. Submitted records and messages cannot be deleted.
- The record revision advances on every accepted save, submission, withdrawal, message and new read acknowledgement. Saves and submissions require the expected revision; delayed responses cannot replace newer case content in the query cache. No-op read acknowledgements and duplicate message retries do not advance it.

Completed and rejected cases express their outcome through the shared status badge and final Progress event; no duplicate outcome banner appears below the timeline.

## Conversations and updates

Package 0.6.0 owns the reusable message-row, date-divider and controlled composer presentation. Student adapters retain the same form and Community API calls, attachment validation, draft stores, send retries, read tracking and preview-resource ownership; this extraction adds no conversation or workflow behavior.

Case details reuse Community message and composer presentation through shared components, with their own form-owned API/query data. A reviewer conversation is not a new Community contact. When unread reviewer updates exist, the Online Forms shell places a warning IssueCard between its title and tabbed panel; selecting it opens My Forms. The My Forms Messages column expresses unseen reviewer updates as warning-coloured singular/plural text rather than a second counter, while the sidebar remains the navigation count. Text-only, attachment-only and combined messages are supported. Limits remain five files, 25 MiB each and 75 MiB total; safe raster images preview inline while other formats remain generic file metadata. There are no ineffective file-delivery buttons or live uploads.

Seeded conversations demonstrate reviewer replies and case outcomes. Newly sent messages appear locally without automatic reviewer replies or approvals. A failed send retains the composer draft. Closed cases have no composer but preserve their history and readable attachments. Form evidence and message attachments remain separate records.

The sidebar badge and shell warning `IssueCard` count distinct owned forms with unseen reviewer messages; My Forms expresses each form's unread total as warning-coloured singular/plural text. Reading a list does not clear an update. A visible, successfully rendered case-message thread acknowledges only the highest reviewer sequence actually displayed; newer unseen updates remain unread. Read watermarks advance monotonically. Student messages and status events do not create reviewer-message counts.

Unsent and sent preview ownership stays in the current session. Removal, draft/session teardown and stale-response cleanup release object URLs without breaking still-visible sent images. Scenario changes clear private caches, drafts and temporary resources; responses from an older generation cannot update the replacement session.

## CAP-15 integration

The [Graduation hub](academic-graduation.md) owns progress while this feature owns clearance/convocation drafts, immutable submissions, history and Messages. Shared `select` fields declare explicit options and optional `requiredWhen` conditions; attending requires a gown size in both UI and API validation. Registry review messages and completed outcomes can be advanced only by the development-only CAP-15 controls. Completed clearance and convocation cases keep Messages open for corrections; other completed/rejected/withdrawn cases retain the existing closed behavior. Convocation participation never gates document collection. Reads accept completed owned enrolments without a current semester, shown as **No current semester** in prefills.

## CAP-50 integration

The [Immigration hub](immigration.md) owns Student Pass availability, progress, invoices and milestone copy. Online Forms owns the new-application, renewal and cancellation drafts, immutable evidence snapshots, status history and case Messages. Submitting one of those catalogue templates atomically creates the matching enrolment-owned `ImmigrationCase`; withdrawal closes its linked active case. A post-cancellation reapplication creates another ordinary New Student Pass submission, snapshots available on-file evidence, and stores missing-record replacements only on that case. After eVAL approval, Immigration Step 4 opens the case-linked Medical Screening Result form. Starting it snapshots the exact case ID; submission atomically attaches immutable file metadata and advances that case to medical review; withdrawal reopens Step 4; staff acceptance completes the form and enables passport handover. Document acceptance, invoice issuance, EMGS/eVAL, medical review, passport, endorsement, expiry and collection milestones remain development-only staff simulations. A graduation-triggered cancellation is created by CAP-15 without a duplicate Online Form because the Graduation Clearance submission already owns that student request.
