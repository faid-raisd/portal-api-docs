Student LMS features (Moodle-like)

Raisd does not ship a separate Moodle product. Student LMS capability is the enrolled-student learning surface on the student portal via Portal API — course content, activities, assessment, communication, and progress — with lecturer/staff write-paths elsewhere. Analogy below is for shape only; CAP IDs and Demo/Live status remain authoritative (SDD-11, SDD-05). Materials alone is still not an LMS (BASE-44).
