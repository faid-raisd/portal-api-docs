1. End-to-end campus lifecycle

One shared MySQL SoR. Staff desks own writes. No thin student/lecturer portal tree and no api_student_* hooks ship in this package. Assist / scholarship is campus-local (templates + b_assist*) — not a government POST like Botswana TEF.
